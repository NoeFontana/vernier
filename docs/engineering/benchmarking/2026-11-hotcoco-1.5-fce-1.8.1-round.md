# 2026-11 baseline refresh — `hotcoco 1.5.0`, `faster-coco-eval 1.8.1`, and `vernier` at HEAD

Full release-mode re-measurement across the entire benchmark matrix at
`b7b160d56be0` on host `a2bed1e4a18e` (`AMD EPYC-Milan Processor`, 4 cores ×
SMT-2 = 8 logical CPUs, 30 GiB RAM, no swap).

What moved since the [2026-10 release 0.6.0 round](./2026-10-release-0.6.0-round.md):

- **`hotcoco` `1.0.1` → `1.5.0`**: major speedups across COCO, LVIS, and
  Objects365, ~3.7× lower peak RSS on RF-DETR Nano, and a new
  `hotcoco.panoptic` (`PanopticEval`) evaluator added in 1.3.0 — wired into the
  matrix as `hotcoco_panoptic`.
- **`faster-coco-eval` `1.8.0` → `1.8.1`**: `_prepare` and annotation-key
  interning optimizations yield ~3–11% faster COCO bbox / segm timings.
- **`ultrafast-pycocotools` (`ufcoco`) `0.1.14`**: no newer PyPI release;
  re-measured unchanged on the same SHA so [`docs/benchmarks.md`](../../benchmarks.md)
  renders from one coherent commit.
- **`vernier` at `b7b160d56be0` (post-0.6.0)**: includes `mimalloc` as the
  extension global allocator (ADR-0070, `c168987`) and the fast PNG label-map
  decoder (ADR-0069, `0d496e8`), dropping panoptic PQ from 10.58 s to 3.68 s
  (−65%), semantic mIoU from 2.89 s to 1.56 s (−46%), and 1-CPU jittered bbox
  from 164.9 ms to 134.3 ms (−19%, overtaking ufcoco's 154.7 ms).
- **Reference oracles unchanged**: `pycocotools==2.0.11`,
  `boundary-iou-api@37d2558`, `panopticapi@7bb4655`, `mmsegmentation@c685fe6`,
  `lvis-api@031ac21`.

  + `c168987` mimalloc). `hotcoco==1.5.0` runs at `7.066 s` (`91 MiB` peak RSS)

- **Semantic mIoU**: `vernier_semantic` dropped from `2.887 s` to `1.556 s`
  (−46%), widening the margin over `mmsegmentation` to `28.60×`.
- **LVIS v1 val bbox**: `vernier_lvis` (`1.045 s`) remains bit-equal to
  `lvis-api` (tensor SHA `5d0a5013f3ec`, 0 divergences; `lvis-api` passed the 5%
  IQR gate cleanly at `2.02%` in this round). `hotcoco_lvis` sped up from
  `4.119 s` to `1.856 s` (−55%, `590 MiB` peak RSS) and reduced its precision
  tensor divergences from `7,069` (`1.0.1`) to `629` (`1.5.0`), but still fails
  `float-tolerance` (`first_divergence` at `[0, 29, 981, 1]`, `1.0` vs
  `0.5714285714285714`, `abs_diff = 0.42857`).

  panoptic (`vernier_panoptic` vs `panopticapi` bit-equal, and `vernier_panoptic`

  `lvis-api` at `2.02%` and `pycocotools` segm at `4.86%`). Only `pycocotools`
  on RF-DETR Nano bbox narrowly exceeded the gate (`5.07%`).
